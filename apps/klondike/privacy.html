By downloading, accessing, or playing the Application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the data collection and processing practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Application.

3. Information We Collect

We collect information in two general ways: (1) information collected automatically through device sensors and software development kits (SDKs), and (2) information you provide to us directly.

A. Information Collected Automatically

When you install and open Klondike Solitaire, certain technical and gameplay information is automatically recorded to ensure the game functions properly:

  • Device & Hardware Identifiers: Operating system version (Android / iOS), device manufacturer, device model, screen resolution, locale/language settings, network connectivity status (Wi-Fi/Cellular via connectivity_plus), battery state, and wake-lock states.
  • Pseudonymous Service Identifiers:
    • Firebase Installation ID (FID): Generated by Firebase to manage remote configurations, error telemetry, and push notifications.
    • Mobile Advertising ID: Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android and Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS (only accessed on iOS if you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework).
  • Gameplay & Progress Metrics: Current game deals, win/loss history, scores, move count, hints and undo actions used, difficulty setting, draw mode (Draw 1 or Draw 3), completion time, and custom card or background selections. This data is primarily stored locally on your device via SQLite / local storage.
  • Diagnostic & Crash Information: Stack traces, memory state, crash logs, and timestamps collected via Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix stability issues.
  • Aggregate Analytics: Session starts, duration, screens viewed, and engagement events tracked via Google Analytics for Firebase.

B. Information You Provide Directly to Us

  • Customer Support Correspondence: If you contact us directly via email (e.g., at brylleseraspe@gmail.com), we receive your email address, name, and any screenshots or details you include in your message. We use this information solely to respond to and resolve your inquiry.
  • In-App Purchases / Subscriptions: If you purchase in-app products (such as "Remove Ads" or virtual subscriptions), transactions are processed directly by the Google Play Store (Google LLC) or the Apple App Store (Apple Inc.). We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses. We only receive a transaction receipt/token confirming purchase status to unlock your feature.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

To ensure high privacy protection, our Application explicitly does NOT collect, request, or store:

  • Precise GPS location data;
  • Biometric data (fingerprints, facial recognition);
  • Contacts, phone books, or call logs;
  • Microphone audio, camera feeds, photos, or media files;
  • Government-issued identification numbers (e.g., SSN, PhilSys Card, Driver's License);
  • User registration credentials or mandatory account passwords (no login is required to play).

Klondike Solitaire integrates industry-standard third-party SDKs provided by Google. These third parties collect and process information subject to their independent privacy policies:

Service / SDK Provider Purpose Data Collected Privacy Link

Google Analytics for Firebase Google LLC Measures user engagement, session length, and feature usage. Firebase Installation ID, app version, OS version, session metrics, interactions. Firebase Privacy Policy

8. Advertising & Tracking Consent Management

Our game displays advertisements via Google AdMob. Depending on your jurisdiction and device settings, you can control how your information is used for advertising:

  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): In regions requiring consent (such as the EEA/UK and applicable US state frameworks), an in-app consent dialog is presented allowing you to accept or manage personalized advertising choices.
  • Apple App Tracking Transparency (iOS): On iOS devices, the app requests explicit permission before accessing the IDFA for tracking across apps. You may decline at any time.
  • Resetting or Opting Out on Android: You can opt out of personalized ads or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time by navigating on your Android device to:
    Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID).
  • Resetting or Opting Out on iOS: You can manage tracking permissions at any time on your Apple device via:
    Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Klondike Solitaire (toggle off).

10. Data Retention & Deletion Rights (Google Play Policy)

In compliance with Google Play's User Data & Data Safety policies, we maintain clear standards for data retention and user deletion requests:

Retention Periods:

  • Local Gameplay Data: Scores, preferences, and game history stored on your device remain until you clear the application data or uninstall the Game.
  • Crash & Diagnostic Logs: Firebase Crashlytics retains crash logs for up to 90 days, after which they are permanently purged.
  • Analytics Data: Google Analytics for Firebase data is retained according to standard Google retention periods (2 to 14 months) before automatic aggregation or deletion.
  • Customer Support Inquiries: Emails sent to customer support are retained for up to 12 months for record-keeping and issue resolution, after which they are deleted.

How to Request Data Deletion:

Your Right to Delete: Because Klondike Solitaire does not require an account, the easiest way to delete your local game data and reset all stored identifiers is to uninstall the application or clear your app data in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Apps > Klondike Solitaire > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • iOS: Delete the Klondike Solitaire application from your device.

If you have communicated with us via email or wish to request deletion of any associated support or diagnostic records, you can submit a deletion request by emailing us at brylleseraspe@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will verify and process your request within thirty (30) calendar days free of charge.

11. Your Rights Under Philippine Law (Data Privacy Act of 2012 / RA 10173)

If you are a resident of the Philippines, your personal data is protected under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). Under the DPA, you have the following rights as a Data Subject:

  • Right to be Informed: You have the right to know whether your personal data is being processed, the sources, categories, purposes, and recipients of such data. This Privacy Policy serves as our notice to you.
  • Right to Access: You have the right to obtain reasonable confirmation and access to your personal data in our possession.
  • Right to Object: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data, including processing for direct marketing, automated profiling, or based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: You have the right to suspend, withdraw, or order the blocking, removal, or destruction of your personal data from our systems if it is incomplete, outdated, unlawfully obtained, or no longer necessary for the declared purpose.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to dispute and correct any inaccuracies in your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Where data is processed by electronic means, you have the right to obtain an electronic copy in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Right to Damages: You have the right to be indemnified for damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of personal data.
  • Right to File a Complaint: If you believe your rights under the Data Privacy Act have been violated or your requests have not been addressed satisfactorily, you have the right to lodge a formal complaint with the National Privacy Commission:
    National Privacy Commission (NPC)
    Website: https://privacy.gov.ph
    Email: info@privacy.gov.ph / complaints@privacy.gov.ph
    Address: 5th Floor Delegation Building, PICC Complex, Vicente Sotto Avenue, Pasay City, Metro Manila 1307, Philippines.

12. Your Rights Under United States Laws (CCPA/CPRA & State Laws)

If you reside in the United States, including California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, or other states with comprehensive consumer privacy laws, this section outlines your specific rights:

A. Notice at Collection & Categories of Information Collected

In the past 12 months, we have collected the following statutory categories of personal information:

  • Identifiers: Pseudonymous device IDs, Google Advertising ID (GAID), Apple IDFA (where permitted), Firebase Installation IDs, IP address.
  • Internet or Network Activity: Gameplay events, time spent, moves count, buttons clicked, and crash diagnostics.
  • Commercial Information: Records of in-app purchase tokens (e.g. "Remove Ads") processed via Google Play / Apple App Store.

B. Notice Regarding "Sale" or "Sharing" of Personal Information

"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information": We do not sell your personal information for monetary compensation. However, under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar state laws, making identifiers (such as Advertising IDs) available to third-party ad networks (like Google AdMob) for cross-context behavioral advertising may be considered "sharing" or a "sale".

You have the absolute right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising. You can exercise this right at any time by:

  • Declining tracking through the in-app consent banner (UMP) or iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt;
  • Disabling personalized ads or deleting your Advertising ID in your Android or iOS device settings (as outlined in Section 8);
  • Contacting us directly by email at brylleseraspe@gmail.com.

C. Your California & US State Consumer Rights

  • Right to Know / Access: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected about you, the categories of sources, and third parties with whom it was shared.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to statutory exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you (e.g., denying game access or altering gameplay quality) for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at brylleseraspe@gmail.com with the subject "US Privacy Request". We will respond within 45 days.
